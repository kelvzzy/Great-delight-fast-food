## The Problems Killing Fast Food Revenue

- **Long Wait Times**: Customers leave queues. They don't come back.
- **Order Errors**: Wrong orders mean remakes, waste, and angry customers
- **No Customer Retention Tool**: People eat and disappear — no way to pull them back
- **Delivery Chaos**: Phone orders, WhatsApp messages, no tracking — a logistical nightmare
- **Inventory Surprises**: Running out of a key ingredient mid-service is a crisis
- **Zero Analytics**: No idea which items are stars, which are dead weight, or when your peak really is

---

## The Solution: Great Relish Smart Restaurant System

### 1. Digital Ordering System
- Touchscreen self-order kiosks at counter (optional)
- QR code table ordering — customers scan, order, pay from their phone
- Online ordering via branded website and app
- Order goes directly to kitchen display — no paper, no shouting, no errors

### 2. Kitchen Display System (KDS)
- Orders appear on screen in the kitchen the moment they're placed
- Priority queue — oldest orders highlighted automatically
- Order status tracking: Received → Preparing → Ready
- Customer gets SMS/app notification: "Your order is ready!"
- Eliminates miscommunication between front and back of house

### 3. Great Relish Loyalty App
- "Relish Points" — earn on every order, redeem for free items
- Push notifications: "It's been a week — here's 20% off your next meal 🍔"
- Birthday deals, flash sales, new menu item alerts
- Digital stamp card — 10 meals, get 1 free
- Referral rewards — bring a friend, both get a discount

### 4. Delivery Management
- Customers order via app for delivery
- Real-time order tracking (like Uber Eats — but yours, no commission)
- Delivery rider assignment and route tracking
- Proof of delivery and customer rating system
- No third-party commission eating your margins

### 5. Restaurant Dashboard
- Real-time sales and order volume
- Best-selling items and dead stock menu analysis
- Peak hour heatmaps — know exactly when to staff up
- Ingredient inventory with low-stock alerts
- Daily/weekly/monthly revenue reports

---

## Futuristic Features (Phase 2 & 3)

- **AI Menu Optimization**: Suggests which items to promote based on margin and popularity
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Slight discounts during slow hours to drive traffic automatically
- **Pre-Order Scheduling**: Customers order for a specific pickup time — zero wait on arrival
- **WhatsApp Ordering Bot**: "Hi Great Relish, I want a burger and chips" — bot processes it
- **Combo Upsell Engine**: "Add a drink for just $0.50?" — automated upsell at checkout
- **Multi-Branch Dashboard**: When Great Relish opens branch 2, manage both from one screen
- **Customer Sentiment Analysis**: AI reads reviews and feedback to flag recurring issues
