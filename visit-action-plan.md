# Great Relish Fast Food — Visit Action Plan

## Pre-Visit Recon
- Visit as a customer during peak hours (lunch 12–2pm) — observe the full experience
- Time how long it takes from order to receiving food
- Note how orders are taken (verbal, written, screen?)
- Check if they have a menu board, printed menu, or digital display
- Look for a loyalty card, stamp card, or any retention mechanism
- Check their social media — are they active? Do they promote deals?

## In-Store Observation Checklist
- [ ] How are orders taken? (Verbal to kitchen, written tickets, POS?)
- [ ] How does the kitchen receive orders? (Shouted, paper, screen?)
- [ ] How long is the average wait time?
- [ ] How are payments handled? (Cash only? Mobile money?)
- [ ] Is there a queue management system or numbering?
- [ ] Do they offer delivery? How is it coordinated?
- [ ] How are daily specials or promotions communicated?
- [ ] Is there a customer feedback mechanism?
- [ ] How is inventory/ingredient stock managed?

## The Approach — Who to Talk To
Target: Owner or Manager (not during lunch rush)
Best time: 10–11:30am (pre-rush) or 3–4:30pm (post-lunch lull)

### Conversation Opener
"Hey, I eat here regularly — the food is genuinely great. I'm with Do'r Stack Software Solutions and we build custom systems for fast food restaurants. I've been watching how things run here and I think there are 2–3 things we could do that would seriously increase your revenue and cut your wait times. Got a few minutes?"

### If They Engage
"The biggest thing killing fast food revenue isn't the food — it's friction. Long queues, order errors, no way to reach customers between visits. We build systems that fix all three: a digital ordering system that cuts wait time in half, a kitchen display that eliminates order errors, and a loyalty app that keeps your customers coming back even when they're not hungry yet."

### The Hook
"A fast food spot we worked with went from 40-minute peak waits to under 15 minutes after we installed a digital ordering and kitchen management system. Their repeat customer rate went up 45% in 3 months from a simple stamp-card loyalty app. That's the kind of result we're talking about for Great Relish."

## Key Pain Points to Probe
1. "What's your biggest challenge during the lunch rush?"
2. "How often do orders come out wrong or get mixed up?"
3. "Do you have a way to reach your regular customers with promotions?"
4. "Are you doing delivery? How's that working?"
5. "How do you know which menu items are your best sellers?"
6. "Do you have a way to manage your ingredient stock before you run out mid-service?"

## Information to Collect
- Average daily orders / covers
- Peak hours and days
- Current ordering and payment system
- Delivery operations (in-house, third-party, none)
- Social media presence and following
- Decision maker identity
- Any plans for expansion or new branches

## Leave-Behind
- Business card
- One-page solution overview for restaurants
- QR code to landing page

## Follow-Up Actions
- [ ] Send tailored proposal within 24hrs
- [ ] Include mockup of their digital menu or loyalty app
- [ ] Schedule kitchen walkthrough assessment
- [ ] Prepare demo of order management flow

## Success Targets
- Minimum: Contact info + agreement to receive proposal
- Target: Kitchen walkthrough scheduled
- Stretch: Agreement to pilot the ordering system for 30 days
